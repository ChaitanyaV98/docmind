# Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Lumen Labs (a fictional company used as sample data for DocMind).

## Annual Leave

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2 days per month, starting from the joining date.

Up to 8 unused days can be carried forward into the next calendar year. Carried-forward days must be used by 31 March, after which they lapse. Unused leave beyond the carry-forward limit is not paid out, except on resignation, where up to 15 accrued days are encashed at the basic salary rate.

Annual leave longer than 5 consecutive working days must be requested at least 3 weeks in advance through the HR portal and approved by your reporting manager.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave does not carry forward. For absences longer than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within 7 days of returning to work.

## Parental Leave

Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken in up to two blocks within the first 12 months after the birth or adoption.

Employees returning from parental leave can work a reduced schedule of 4 days per week for up to 3 months, at full pay, with manager approval.

## Public Holidays

The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The list is published on the HR portal every December. Employees who are required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off, which must be used within 60 days.

## Unpaid Leave

After using all paid leave, employees may request up to 30 days of unpaid leave per year. Unpaid leave requires approval from both the reporting manager and the HR business partner.
