# Expense Policy

Lumen Labs (a fictional company used as sample data for DocMind) reimburses reasonable business expenses that are submitted with receipts within 30 days of being incurred.

## Submitting Claims

Submit every claim through the Expensify workspace with an itemised receipt. Claims above ₹5,000 require a short business justification. Claims submitted more than 30 days after the expense will be rejected unless the finance team grants an exception.

Approved claims are reimbursed with the next payroll cycle, provided they are approved before the 20th of the month.

## Travel

Domestic flights must be booked in economy class through the company travel desk at least 14 days before departure where possible. International flights longer than 8 hours may be booked in premium economy.

Hotel stays are reimbursed up to ₹6,000 per night in metro cities and ₹4,000 per night elsewhere. Local travel by cab is reimbursed at actual cost; personal vehicle use is reimbursed at ₹12 per kilometre.

## Meals and Per Diem

While travelling on business, employees receive a per diem of ₹1,500 per day for meals, which does not require receipts. Client entertainment is reimbursed at actual cost up to ₹3,000 per person and must list the attendees.

Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client events.

## Equipment and Home Office

Remote employees receive a one-time home-office allowance of ₹25,000 for a desk, chair and accessories. Laptops and monitors are provided by IT and remain company property.

A monthly internet allowance of ₹1,000 is paid with salary to all employees who work remotely at least 2 days per week.

## Learning Budget

Each employee has an annual learning budget of ₹40,000 for courses, books, certifications and conference tickets. Unused learning budget does not roll over to the next year.
